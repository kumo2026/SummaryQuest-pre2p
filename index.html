  1. Topic(テーマ)
  2. Good point（良いところ）

① Shorter words! (短く縮める)

More and more people ➔ More people/Many people

some / many students ➔ students

at home and at school ➔ anywhere

whenever they want ➔ anytime

lions and elephants ➔ animals

dogs and cats ➔ pets

reading and writing ➔ study skills

busy life & homework ➔ daily life

sports and exercise ➔ activities

rivers, mountains ➔ nature

② Change the sentence! (文型チェンジ)

● If you use AI effectively, it helps people learn faster.
➔ Using AI helps people learn faster.

● E-books were read by many students.
➔ Many students read e-books.

● People speak English in many countries.
➔ English is spoken in many countries.

● People think AI helps them in work and study.
➔ AI is helpful for work and study.

● Many students think group study is useful.
➔ Group study is useful.

● People are using smartphones to watch videos such as YouTube and TikTok.
➔ Watching videos on smartphones is becoming popular.

● Camping gives people a chance to enjoy nature.
➔ Camping lets people enjoy nature.

● Online classes give students opportunities to study at home or while traveling.
➔ Online classes allow students to study anywhere.

● It is often said that too much homework causes students to feel tired.
➔ Too much homework makes students feel tired.

● Some people say that social media causes stress for young people.
➔ Social media can make young people stressed.

● More and more people are riding bicycles.
➔ More people ride bicycles.

● More and more students study online.
➔ More students study online.

③ Use short words (短い接続詞)
However Also and or because so if when
