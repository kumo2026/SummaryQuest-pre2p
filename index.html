  2. Good point（良いところ）
  3. Problem（問題点）
① Shorter words! (短く縮める)

More and more people ➔ People

Many people ➔ People

some / many students ➔ students

at home and at school ➔ anywhere

whenever they want ➔ anytime

lions and elephants ➔ animals

dogs and cats ➔ pets

reading and writing ➔ study skills

busy life & homework ➔ daily life

sports and exercise ➔ activities

rivers, mountains ➔ nature

② Change the sentence! (文型チェンジ)

If ➔ Using

was used by ➔ use

People find ➔ It is

give ➔ let / allow

cause ➔ make

More and more ➔ More

● If you use AI, it helps people learn.
➔ Using AI helps people learn.

● The book was read by many students.
➔ Many students read the book.

● People find AI helpful.
➔ It is helpful.

● Camping gives people a chance to enjoy nature.
➔ Camping lets people enjoy nature.

● Online classes give students opportunities to study anywhere.
➔ Online classes allow students to study anywhere.

● Too much homework causes students to feel tired.
➔ Too much homework makes students feel tired.

● More and more people are riding bicycles.
➔ More people ride bicycles.

③ Use short words (短い接続詞)
However Also and because so
④ Cut! Cut!(バッサリ消す)
  • For example / such as の後ろ
  • 具体的な 数・場所・名前
  • very / really / quite / a lot of
  • 細かくて長い説明（ディテール）
